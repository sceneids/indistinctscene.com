after the rain

The street is quieter after rain, even when the traffic has returned. Every surface carries a darker version of itself, and the air has a temporary edge.

Someone has opened a window across the way. A bus folds light into the puddles. There is nowhere particular to go, which makes the walk feel complete.

By morning, most of it will be ordinary again. For now, the city is still listening.
