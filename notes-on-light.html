notes on light

There is a point in the evening when a room stops being a room and becomes a collection of surfaces. A glass on the table. A sleeve on the chair. The pale square of a window holding on to the last part of the day.

Nothing in it asks to be noticed. That is probably why it is easy to stay with. Light moves slowly across the floor, changing a familiar object into something almost unnamed.

Later, when the room is dark, the memory is less about what was there than how little was needed.
