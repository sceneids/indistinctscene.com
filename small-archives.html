small archives

A ticket stub can survive several moves. So can a note written quickly enough that the handwriting becomes part of the weather of the day.

These things are not evidence of anything useful. They are small interruptions: a color, a place name, the price of coffee, a date that feels both exact and unimportant.

Kept together, they make a gentle argument for looking back without trying to reconstruct the whole story.
